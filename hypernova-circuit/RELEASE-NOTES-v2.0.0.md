# Hypernova Circuit v2.0.0 — Grand Circuit Upgrade

Hypernova Circuit 2.0 replaces the flat/repetitive track feel with a much larger, continuously curving Grand Circuit built for longer, more readable racing sessions.

## Major changes

- Six named Grand Circuit environments with 2.4 km sectors.
- Four checkpoint splits inside every sector.
- Continuous sweeping bends, S-curves and track banking generated from a non-resetting course path.
- Wider raceway and more room to choose racing lines.
- Automatic drift system: hard steering at speed builds Drift Charge, restores nitro and banks bonus coins when the drift ends cleanly.
- Warp boost lanes that grant speed, nitro and coins.
- Rival packs instead of treating every competitor as a generic obstacle.
- Overtake tracking and rewards.
- Seven encounter families with deterministic variety and readable spacing.
- Checkpoint arches and stronger course landmarks.
- Four player cars now have different silhouettes as well as different colors/stats.
- Expanded HUD for circuit name, checkpoint progress, overtakes and drift charge.
- Cache-safe PWA versioning and expanded portal SEO manifest.

Existing local save data remains compatible because the v1 save key and progression schema are preserved.
