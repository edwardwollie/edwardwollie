# Wildfront Horizon 2.0.1 — Shot Feedback Fix

This release makes every shot outcome immediately readable.

- Actual animal contact now produces a bright world-space impact pulse.
- Non-vital contact produces an amber hit marker, recoil, and explicit HIT feedback.
- Clean vital contact produces a larger lime hit marker and a short physical knockdown before the animal leaves the scene.
- Misses now display a brief red MISS marker.
- Existing mission balance, wildlife AI, progression, saves, port 8096, and health endpoint are unchanged.
