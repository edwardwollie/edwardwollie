# Wildfront Horizon 2.0.0 — Living Reserve Upgrade

## Major upgrades
- Expanded from 8 to 12 missions and from 3 to 4 core reserves.
- Added Bighorn Sheep and Bison with distinct procedural body silhouettes.
- Reworked wildlife into herd-aware AI with graze, walk, alert and flee states.
- Wildlife now leaves fresh dynamic tracks while moving instead of relying on one static trail.
- Trail Scanner highlights fresh sign and wildlife subtly rather than turning animals into bright targets.
- Scope system now cycles 1x / 3x / 6x and shows range, wind, target state and tracked species.
- Added hold-breath / steady-aim stamina with Shift on desktop and STEADY on touch.
- Clean-shot logic now favors patient chest shots and penalizes uncertain angles.
- Expanded reserve dressing with brush, rock fields, water features and distant ridge landmarks.
- Added more weather-specific fog behavior and stronger time-of-day lighting variation.
- Added a procedural rifle silhouette and redesigned HUD for clearer field feedback.
- Preserved local save migration, field-kit upgrades, mobile support, port 8096 and `wildfront-ok` health response.
