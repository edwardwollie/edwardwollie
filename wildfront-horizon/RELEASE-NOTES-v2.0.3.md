# Wildfront Horizon v2.0.3 — Shot Grade Update

- Head and muzzle contacts now count as vital hits.
- PERFECT: headshot vital hit, highest score tier.
- GREAT: chest, shoulder, or visible upper-torso vital hit.
- GOOD: confirmed body hit; animal immediately staggers before fleeing.
- Removed the old headshot penalty/message.
- Accuracy now measures registered hits divided by shots fired; clean vital harvests remain tracked separately.
- Mission results show PERFECT, GREAT, and GOOD shot counts.
- Existing missions, saves, herds, optics, tracking, controls, hostname and port are preserved.
