# Spaceflight Academy v2.1.0 — Variety Rush

## Kid-friendly Space Rush

- Replaced the active 5×5 WASD square field with a continuous three-lane flight course.
- Six space-science answer gates per mission.
- Correct gate: barrier splits open and the rocket flies through.
- Wrong gate: visible crash-through feedback, correct explanation, and immediate continuation.
- No wrong answer restarts the mission.

## Learning variety

Each age path now has 72 encounters across six topics:

1. Forces & Motion
2. Rocket Systems
3. Planets & Moons
4. Orbits & Gravity
5. Navigation & Communication
6. Space Safety & Science

Every six-gate mission contains all six topics. Missions 1–12 consume all 72 age-specific encounters before an exact question repeats.

## Timing standard

- Narration reads the question and all three choices before timing begins.
- 7-second thinking grace after narration.
- Answer windows: 30 / 28 / 26 / 24 / 22 seconds by difficulty tier.
- READ AGAIN restarts narration + grace + the full answer window.

## Preserved systems

Rocket building, 90-mission progression, sector bosses, family activities, age paths, rocket-part guide, local save data, stars/XP/star cores, touch/keyboard support, and offline PWA support remain.
